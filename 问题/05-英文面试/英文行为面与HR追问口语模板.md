## 5. STAR Frameworks

### 5.1 中文 STAR 骨架

> 当时的背景是 **[真实系统、用户和约束]**，我的明确责任是 **[本人 RACI]**。真正困难的地方是 **[冲突、未知或风险]**。我先做了 **[动作一]**，然后做了 **[动作二]**；在 **[取舍点]** 上，我根据 **[数据、实验或规则]** 选择了 **[方案]**，最终由 **[真实决策人]** 决定。结果是 **[可核验结果，也可以是失败或延期]**，证据是 **[记录]**。我做得不够的是 **[反思]**，后来把 **[流程、测试或设计]** 改成了 **[变化]**。

### 5.2 English STAR Skeleton

> The situation was `[verified context and constraint]`. My specific responsibility was `[my RACI, not the whole team's work]`. The main challenge was `[conflict, uncertainty, or risk]`. I first `[action one]`, then `[action two]`. At the key trade-off, I used `[data, experiment, or rule]` and recommended `[option]`. The final decision belonged to `[real owner]`. The result was `[verifiable result, including a failure or delay]`. What I would improve is `[reflection]`, and I later changed `[process, test, or design]`.

### 5.3 Honest Fallback When I Do Not Have the Experience

> I do not have a directly comparable experience that I can verify. The closest real example is `[smaller case]`. I can explain what I personally did in that case, and I can also explain how I would approach your scenario. I do not want to present a design answer as a production incident or a leadership story.

## 6. Six-Step English Project Answer

1. **Problem**: "The problem was `[user and pain]`."
2. **Current system**: "The current implementation works by `[short end-to-end chain]`."
3. **My verified scope**: "I was responsible for `[modules and stages]`; `[roles]` handled `[other scope]`."
4. **One decision**: "The key trade-off was `[A versus B]`, and we chose `[choice]` because `[evidence]`."
5. **Evidence**: "The result we can support is `[metric, artifact, or delivered capability]`, measured by `[method]`."
6. **Limitation**: "It still does not solve `[boundary]`. If the requirement grows, I would `[next design]`."
